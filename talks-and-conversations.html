Talks and Conversations

Talks and Conversations

I learn through conversation. Much of my work has happened in classrooms, workshops, podcasts, panels, conference rooms and radio studios. It has also happened in informal talks with people who want to rethink education.

This page collects talks, podcasts, interviews and public conversations from France, Pakistan and the United States. Some are in languages other than English. I chose recordings with subtitles wherever they exist.

New interview

CEPI and Wellness

The Wellness Octave with Dr. Kell Fujimoto · Episode 23 · October 2026

Dr. Kell Fujimoto and I discuss how teaching practices, classroom environments and faculty-student relationships affect student wellness. CEPI treats wellness as one of six pillars of student support. We also talk about UDL, trauma-informed teaching and how AI can support teaching while people stay at the center.

Podcasts

Most of these podcasts discuss AI, faculty development and the future of higher education. Many draw on experiments at SFBU, including the APP curriculum and its ten “How To” courses. They also cover the work with Alma Learning, custom AI teaching assistants, authentic assessment and human-centered course design.

Interviews and Talks

Many of these talks come from my years as a founding faculty member at Habib University, the first liberal arts and sciences university in Pakistan. They discuss how to rethink higher education and build new institutional cultures. They cover The Playground, the first design research lab in the country, and the Center for Pedagogical Excellence, which supported teaching, learning and faculty development.
