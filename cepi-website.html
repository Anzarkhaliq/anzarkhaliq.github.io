Teaching and Learning at SFBU

A website that turns a 54-page faculty guide into short pages a teacher can read between classes.

The CEPI Website
  1. Why a website
  2. The pages
  3. And the Canvas Hub

The Teaching and Learning Guide is 54 pages long. Few faculty will read 54 pages in one sitting, and they should not have to. The website breaks the guide into short pages, one for each part of our practice. They cover what we believe, PERS, Think, Do, Create, course design, teaching in the room, trauma-aware teaching, AI and assessment.

Each page starts with the idea in a sentence. Then it gives examples from four disciplines, a short exercise for the first two weeks of term and a list of further reading. A faculty member can find what they need in a minute and go deeper when they have time. I designed it to sit inside SFBU’s main website, in the university’s own colors and type.

And the Canvas Hub

The website is for reading. The CEPI Canvas Hub is for working, with the templates, tools and recordings faculty use while they build a course.
