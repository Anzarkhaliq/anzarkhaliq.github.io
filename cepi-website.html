CEPI Website

A website built for SFBU faculty and for educators outside the university who want to see how SFBU teaches.

Teaching and Learning at SFBU

Teaching and Learning at SFBU is a website my team and I designed at CEPI for two groups of readers. SFBU faculty use it to find our frameworks, services and support. Visitors from outside the university use it to see how SFBU approaches teaching and learning.

The Website and the Canvas Hub

The website is for reading. The CEPI Canvas Hub is for working: templates, tools and recordings faculty use while they build a course.
