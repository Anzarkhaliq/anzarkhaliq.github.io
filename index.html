Teaching Now

Fall 2026 · San Francisco Bay University
APP 301

How Can We Thrive? Scientific Inquiry and the Future of Sustainability

Fall 2026Term
SFBUUniversity
BayleyAI companion

I am teaching this course at SFBU in Fall 2026. Before class each week, every student holds a one-on-one conversation with Bayley. Bayley is an AI video companion built with Alma Learning around that week’s sustainability question. It asks each student to take a position, defend it and answer the strongest objection.

The conversations produce a class report that I receive every Monday. The report shows where students agree, where their reasoning is thin and which scientific ideas need a second look. I redesign the Tuesday session from that report, so class time goes to the questions students could not settle alone.

About

The longer story · CV

I trained as an experimental physicist. In 2014 I joined Habib University in Karachi as a founding faculty member. Habib was the first liberal arts and sciences university in Pakistan.

At Habib I founded the Integrated Sciences and Mathematics program. I co-founded the Center for Transdisciplinarity, Design and Innovation. Later I became the founding Associate Dean for Teaching and Learning. My work there covered curriculum, faculty development, learning experiences and the systems that connect them.

Today I am the first Chief Learning Officer at San Francisco Bay University (SFBU). I work on AI-integrated learning, faculty development, curriculum redesign and the design of human-centered educational systems.

At SFBU I helped shape a new general education model built on ten “How To” courses. These courses make the hidden curriculum visible. They teach durable human capabilities that universities value and seldom write down.

My work draws on trauma-informed teaching and research on neurodivergence. It also draws on culturally sustaining practices, metacognitive learning, authentic assessment and relationship-rich pedagogy. I test new forms of AI-supported learning alongside them.

One belief connects these roles. Education should cross disciplinary boundaries and leave behind fixed content delivery and industrial-era assumptions about learning. This site collects the courses, workshops, talks, letters and essays that came from that belief.
