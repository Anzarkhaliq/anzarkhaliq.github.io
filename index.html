About

About me · CV

I came to education through physics. My doctorate at Sorbonne University took me to synchrotron beamlines, where I spent long nights measuring how materials behave at the scale of atoms. Along the way I hosted a rock and metal radio show and trained in theater and improvisation. Those two worlds kept asking me the same question: what happens to people when they share a room and try to understand something new?

That question brought me to Habib University in Karachi in 2014, as a founding faculty member of the first liberal arts and sciences university in Pakistan. Over ten years I founded its Integrated Sciences and Mathematics program and its Center for Pedagogical Excellence, and co-founded a design lab called The Playground. I also served as its founding Associate Dean for Teaching and Learning.

Today I am the first Chief Learning Officer at San Francisco Bay University, where I lead the Center for Empowerment and Pedagogical Innovation (CEPI). My work sits where three questions meet. How do people learn? How can an institution be designed around that learning? What changes now that AI sits in every classroom?

Background

From the beamline to the classroom

I trained in physics, hosted a radio show and performed improvisation. Each of these taught me something about how people pay attention, take risks and understand one another. Together they shaped the way I design learning.

Much of my work ultimately revolves around a simple but difficult question: how can educational systems remain deeply human while also adapting honestly to technological, cultural, and societal change?

Explore My Work

Everything in one place

Most of my recent work happens at CEPI, the Center for Empowerment and Pedagogical Innovation, which I lead at San Francisco Bay University. CEPI helps SFBU faculty design and teach their courses. Earlier work happened at Habib University in Karachi, where I spent ten years building programs and a design lab.

01 · Courses · SFBU and Habib University

Teaching Experiments

Five courses I designed to test my own ideas with real students. They run from a renewable energy course in Karachi to the course I teach at SFBU this fall.

02 · Canvas · SFBU

LMS Designs

Canvas sites I design so that students and faculty can find their way through a course. They include course templates for SFBU’s MBA Pro+ programs and the CEPI Canvas Hub.

03 · Faculty guide · CEPI, SFBU

The Teaching and Learning Guide

A 54-page guide my team and I wrote for SFBU faculty. This page shares its frameworks for readers outside the university.

04 · Website · CEPI, SFBU

The CEPI Website

Teaching and Learning at SFBU, a website that breaks the guide into short pages a faculty member can read between classes.

05 · Faculty institute · CEPI, SFBU

Roots & Branches

The faculty institute I run every August. Faculty bring a course they are about to teach and leave with materials they built in the room.

06 · Design research lab · Habib University, Karachi

The Playground at Habib

The first design research lab in Pakistan, which I founded and directed. Its journal records the courses, workshops and research projects that ran through it.

07 · Weekly letter · CEPI, SFBU

Weekend Reads

A letter on teaching I write for SFBU faculty every Friday. Each edition takes one classroom problem and ends with something to try the next week.
