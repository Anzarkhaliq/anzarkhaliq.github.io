Teaching and Learning at SFBU

A 54-page guide to teaching practice for SFBU faculty, with examples from SFBU courses. CEPI published version 4.0 in September 2026.

The guide
  1. Premise
  2. Five frameworks
  3. The Pyramid After AI
  4. Figures
  5. Nine sections

The guide starts from one premise. A university education should change how a person sees the world and what that person believes they can do in it. Academic knowledge is the means to that end.

Each section turns research on teaching into practice. Frameworks come with examples from SFBU courses in supply chain management, psychology, engineering and other fields. Callout boxes give faculty actions to take in the first two weeks of a term.

I lead the CEPI work behind the guide. A web edition of the guide has also been prepared for the SFBU website.

Five Frameworks, Five Questions

Each framework answers a different design question at a different level, from the institution down to one assignment.

FrameworkThe question it answersWhere it works
The Six PillarsWhat does SFBU commit to for every student?The institution
PERSWhat qualities should any learning experience have?Every course and session
Think / Do / CreateHow should learning be structured and sequenced?Units, sessions and assignments
Bloom’s Taxonomy and the Pyramid After AIWhat kind of thinking, and which human capacities, should the work demand?Learning outcomes and assessment
The New Learning CycleWhat path should one assignment take students through, now that AI is in the room?Individual assignments

The Pyramid After AI

The Pyramid After AI is a CEPI framework for the human capacities that stay essential when AI handles much of the old work of learning. It sits inverted above Bloom’s Taxonomy. The two meet where AI output becomes the starting point for human work.

Six capacities rise from that point. Students use them in cycles, and they return to earlier capacities as their understanding grows.

1Decode

Do I understand the output?

2Judge

Is it good, true or appropriate?

3Shape

Does it make sense, and have I reshaped it?

4Author

Does my thinking, voice and judgment show?

5Transfer

Can I apply this learning in a new situation?

6Become

What changed in me as a learner or human being?
