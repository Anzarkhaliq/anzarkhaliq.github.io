The Playground
  1. What it was

  3. The journal

The Playground was the first design research lab in Pakistan. I started it at Habib University so that students and faculty from any discipline could work on real problems together. They worked with their hands and with the methods of design research. Its full name says what it tried to do: the Center for Transdisciplinarity, Design and Innovation.

The lab worked in four areas: teaching, training, design research, and the spaces and materials people needed to make things. Courses ran through it in computer science, engineering, social development and policy, and communication and design. Students ran short workshops for one another. Faculty redesigned their courses there with the Center for Pedagogical Excellence. Partners such as Karachi Electric, the World Bank and the Sindh Education Foundation brought their own problems for students and faculty to work on.

Five values guided the work.

Human and planet-centeredCollaborationDiverse perspectivesLearning by doingIterating

One student research project, Mapping Festivity, studied how Karachi’s wedding halls changed over a decade and traced the supply chains behind them. It was shown at the 17th Venice Architecture Biennale.
