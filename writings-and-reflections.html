Writings and Reflections

I write to think through questions about learning, institutions, AI, design, science, creativity and human experience. Each piece records reflections, tensions and experiments as they develop. Most stop short of a final answer.

Like the courses and workshops on this site, the essays cross disciplines. They draw on education, systems thinking, philosophy, design and lived experience. Weekly letters for SFBU faculty are in Weekend Reads.
