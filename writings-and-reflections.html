Writing & Reflections

Writing has become another space for me to think through questions surrounding learning, institutions, AI, design, science, creativity, and human experience.

These pieces are less about arriving at final answers and more about documenting evolving reflections, tensions, experiments, and ways of seeing.

Much like the courses and workshops on this site, the writing often moves across disciplinary boundaries and sits somewhere between education, systems thinking, philosophy, design, and lived experience.

Book manuscript

How Can We Thrive?

Scientific Inquiry and the Future of Sustainability. I am writing it alongside the course I teach at SFBU. The manuscript is under revision and will be published this fall.

Weekly letter

Weekend Reads

Every Friday I write Weekend Reads, a letter on teaching for SFBU faculty. Each edition takes one classroom problem, works through the research on it and ends with something to try the next week. The archive is open to everyone.
